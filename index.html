How to Play

🕹️ Arcade Mode

Pick a curated zone or search/browse the global hotspot index for a real eBird location. Eight birds appear on a grid at once, each on its own countdown — click one and type its name before time runs out. Wrong guesses and timeouts cost a life; find as many species as you can before they run out.

⚡ Blitz Mode

A race-the-clock Big Day: no lives, just a single master countdown. Identify as many species as possible before time runs out, and spend time to travel to new hotspots along the way — farther trips cost more of the clock. Every species you catch here also counts toward your lifetime stats, achievements, and Monthly Bingo goals, same as Arcade.

📖 Practice Mode

An untimed, infinite study mode. Filter by bird group and/or region — or leave both blank to draw from every species in the database — and identify birds at your own pace. End the session any time to see your accuracy and a list of what you missed.

🐦 Bird of the Day

A new species is featured every day. Type your best guess before revealing the answer, use the hint to see the general region it's been recorded in without giving away exact hotspots, and keep your streak going by getting it right day after day.

🎲 Monthly Bingo

A fresh 25-goal board is generated at the start of every month, the same board for everyone. Complete goals by identifying matching species in Arcade or Blitz — a family, a country, a name pattern — and fully completing the board earns a special glowing border and counts toward the Bingo Fanatic achievement.

🏆 Trophy Case & Stats

Tracks your lifetime totals, rarest finds, high scores, and tiered achievements — all saved locally to this browser.

Your life list, trophy case, high scores, and achievements are all saved locally to this browser only — switching devices, clearing browser data, or the game moving to a new hosting URL will lose them unless you back up first.
